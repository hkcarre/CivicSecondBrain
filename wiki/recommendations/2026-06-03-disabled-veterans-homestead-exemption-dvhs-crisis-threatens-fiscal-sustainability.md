## AI ANALYSIS — Requires Council Review

**Finding:** The DVHS exemption now represents $7.08 billion in exempt property value, causing $5.5 million in annual revenue loss (19% of total property tax collections). State reimbursement covers only 17.5% of claims statewide, leaving Schertz to absorb 82.5% of the impact—a structural deficit growing at 8.6% annually.

**Evidence:**
- DVHS exemptions grew 8.6% in FY2025-26, down from a three-year average of 22% annual growth [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26, topics/governance.md]
- Total exempt property value: $7.08 billion; annual revenue loss: $5.5 million (19% of property tax collections) [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26, topics/governance.md]
- State DVHS aid program currently funds only 17.5% of total requests statewide [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26, topics/governance.md]
- Property tax rate increased to $0.5118 per $100 in FY2025-26 (1.1% increase) despite DVHS headwinds [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26, topics/budget.md]
- General Fund balance fell to 25% of operating expenditures in FY2024-25, below the 26% policy target [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2024-25, topics/budget.md]

**Comparable Cities:**
- Data unavailable for Cibolo, New Braunfels, or San Marcos DVHS impact. Regional comparison needed to assess whether Schertz's 19% revenue loss is typical for fast-growing military-adjacent cities.

**Suggested Action:** Direct City Manager to prepare a three-scenario fiscal model (8%, 15%, 22% annual DVHS growth) through FY2030, quantifying impacts on fund balance, debt capacity, and service levels. Simultaneously, join a coalition of Texas cities to lobby the Legislature for full DVHS reimbursement in the 2027 session. Consider a public education campaign explaining the unfunded mandate's impact on city services.

**Council Discussion Questions:**
- At what DVHS growth rate does Schertz's fund balance fall below the 25% emergency threshold, and what services would need to be cut?
- Should the city pursue a voter-approved tax rate increase now to build reserves, or wait until the DVHS impact forces cuts?
- What is the regional legislative strategy to secure state reimbursement, and which cities should Schertz partner with?
