## AI ANALYSIS — Requires Council Review

**Finding:** Sales tax revenue growth decelerated from 10% (FY2023) to 3.5% (FY2023-24) to projected 6% (FY2024-25), while SEDC Fund revenue declined from $10.05M (FY2022-23) to $8.93M (FY2025-26). No retail recruitment strategy, target industry analysis, or fiscal impact studies are documented in Strategic Plan or budget materials.

**Evidence:**
- Sales tax revenue grew 10% in FY2023, projected 7% growth for FY2024 [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2023-24, topics/development.md]
- Sales tax revenue growth projected at 6% for FY2024-25 after slowing to 3.5% in FY2023-24 [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2024-25, topics/budget.md]
- SEDC Fund revenues: $10,050,960 (FY2022-23) → $8,407,000 (FY2023-24) → $8,343,000 (FY2024-25) → $8,928,608 (FY2025-26) [SOURCE: Multiple budget documents, topics/development.md]
- City of Schertz charges 1% sales tax and Economic Development charges 0.5% sales tax [SOURCE: Schertz Tax Rates, topics/governance.md]
- Median household income: $100,325; 43% of residents have college degrees [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26, topics/governance.md]

**Comparable Cities:**
- New Braunfels (adjacent I-35 corridor) has robust retail sector including outlet malls. Schertz may be experiencing sales tax 'leakage' to New Braunfels and San Antonio. Comparative retail sales per capita analysis needed.

**Suggested Action:** Direct SEDC to produce a Target Industry Study by December 2026 identifying retail, professional services, and light industrial sectors aligned with Schertz's demographics (median income $100K, 43% college-educated). Conduct a 'buy local' campaign measuring sales tax leakage to adjacent cities. Adopt an Economic Development Strategic Plan with 5-year sales tax growth targets, retail recruitment metrics, and quarterly SEDC performance reporting to Council. Consider retail incentive policy for strategic sites (e.g., FM 1518 corridor).

**Council Discussion Questions:**
- What is the total taxable sales per capita in Schertz vs. New Braunfels vs. Cibolo, and where is the gap?
- Should SEDC prioritize retail/commercial recruitment or focus on high-wage employment that indirectly boosts sales tax through resident spending?
- What percentage of Schertz residents' retail spending occurs outside city limits (sales tax leakage), and which categories (dining, auto, big-box) show the largest gaps?
