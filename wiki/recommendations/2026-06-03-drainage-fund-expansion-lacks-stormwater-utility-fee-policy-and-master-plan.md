## AI ANALYSIS — Requires Council Review

**Finding:** Drainage Fund adds 6 new positions in FY2025-26 and revenue grows to $2.3M, but no stormwater master plan, fee methodology, or capital improvement prioritization is documented. Wendy Swan Drainage Improvements ($1.1M) and FM 1518 City Park upgrades ($45K) appear reactive rather than system-planned.

**Evidence:**
- Drainage Fund Revenue: $2,316,283 (FY2025-26) vs. $1,445,564 (FY2023-24)—59% growth [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26 vs. FY2023-24, topics/infrastructure.md]
- Budget adds 6 Drainage positions in FY2025-26 [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26, topics/governance.md]
- Wendy Swan Drainage Improvements: $1,100,000 [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26, topics/budget.md]
- FM 1518 City Park Underground Drainage Upgrade: $45,000 [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26, topics/budget.md]
- No stormwater master plan, drainage basin analysis, or utility fee structure documented in any budget FY2020-2026

**Comparable Cities:**
- San Antonio has a dedicated Stormwater Utility Fee ($5–15/month per property). Austin maintains a Watershed Protection Master Plan with prioritized capital projects. Schertz lacks both.

**Suggested Action:** Commission a Stormwater Master Plan by June 2027 including: (1) drainage basin inventory and hydraulic modeling; (2) 20-year capital improvement needs assessment; (3) development impact fee recommendations; (4) stormwater utility fee feasibility study. Adopt a Drainage CIP prioritization methodology (e.g., flood risk scoring, cost-benefit analysis). If implementing a stormwater utility fee, conduct public engagement to explain the 'user pays' model. Report Drainage Fund performance quarterly given rapid growth.

**Council Discussion Questions:**
- Should Schertz implement a stormwater utility fee ($/month per property) to create a stable, growth-independent revenue stream for drainage infrastructure?
- What is the current level of service for drainage (e.g., design storm = 25-year event), and is it adequate for climate change projections?
- How much of the Drainage Fund growth is driven by new development impact fees vs. General Fund transfers, and is the funding model sustainable?
