## AI ANALYSIS — Requires Council Review

**Finding:** Despite population growth from 35,121 (FY2019) to 43,969 (FY2025-26), street maintenance allocations remain inconsistent: $650K (FY2025-26) vs. $1M (FY2024-25) vs. $3.1M (FY2023-24). A $20M Certificate of Obligation for streets was issued in 2024, but no pavement management system or multi-year capital plan is documented.

**Evidence:**
- Street Maintenance (General Fund): $650,000 in FY2025-26 [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26, topics/budget.md]
- Street maintenance allocation: $1 million in FY2024-25 [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2024-25, topics/development.md]
- Street Maintenance and Repair Budget: $3,118,351 in FY2023-24 [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2023-24, topics/budget.md]
- $20 million Certificates of Obligation to be issued for street projects in September 2024 [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2024-25, topics/budget.md]
- City serves 38.22 square miles (FY2025-26), up from 32 square miles (FY2022-23) [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26 vs. FY2022-23]

**Comparable Cities:**
- Data unavailable. Recommend benchmarking New Braunfels (similar growth rate, I-35 corridor) and Cibolo (adjacent city, shared road network) for street maintenance spending per lane-mile.

**Suggested Action:** Commission a Pavement Management System (PMS) study by March 2027 to inventory all city streets, assess condition (PCI ratings), and model 20-year replacement costs. Establish a dedicated Street Preservation Fund with annual transfers of 1.5–2% of General Fund revenue. Adopt a policy that all new development pays proportional street impact fees based on PMS lifecycle costs. Report annually to Council on pavement condition index trends.

**Council Discussion Questions:**
- What is the current average pavement condition index (PCI) for Schertz streets, and what PCI level triggers reconstruction vs. maintenance?
- Should the city pursue a voter-approved street bond in 2027 to address deferred maintenance, or continue pay-as-you-go with Certificates of Obligation?
- How much of the FY2024 $20M street bond will be consumed by new growth areas vs. existing neighborhood maintenance?
