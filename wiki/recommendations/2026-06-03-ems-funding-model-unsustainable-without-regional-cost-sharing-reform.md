## AI ANALYSIS — Requires Council Review

**Finding:** EMS Fund operates across a 220-square-mile service area spanning multiple jurisdictions (Schertz, Cibolo, Seguin/Guadalupe County) but cost-sharing mechanisms appear ad-hoc. Bad debt expense of $2.5M (FY2020-21) suggests revenue collection challenges, and budget adds 4 EMS positions in FY2025-26 without documented interlocal agreement updates.

**Evidence:**
- EMS Fund total revenues: $17,060,386 (FY2025-26) vs. $15,070,944 (FY2023-24)—17% growth [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26 vs. FY2023-24, topics/budget.md]
- EMS adding 8 new positions to serve 220 square mile service area (FY2022-23) [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2022-23, topics/development.md]
- EMS bad debt expense: $2,510,227 in FY2020-21 [SOURCE: FY 2020-21 Adopted Budget, topics/infrastructure.md]
- Schertz support payment for EMS: $667,049; Cibolo: $473,115; Seguin/Guadalupe County: $843,577 (FY2020-21) [SOURCE: FY 2020-21 Adopted Budget, topics/infrastructure.md]
- Budget adds 4 EMS positions in FY2025-26 [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26, topics/governance.md]

**Comparable Cities:**
- Regional EMS funding models vary widely. Austin-Travis County EMS uses a taxing district; San Antonio Fire has integrated EMS. Schertz's multi-jurisdictional model requires formal cost-allocation methodology.

**Suggested Action:** Renegotiate interlocal agreements with Cibolo and Seguin/Guadalupe County by June 2027, adopting a transparent cost-allocation formula based on: (1) call volume by jurisdiction; (2) assessed property value; (3) population served. Implement advanced life support (ALS) billing optimization to reduce bad debt below 10% of gross revenue. Explore formation of an Emergency Services District (ESD) to create a dedicated funding stream. Report quarterly EMS financial performance to Council.

**Council Discussion Questions:**
- What percentage of EMS calls originate in Schertz vs. Cibolo vs. unincorporated Guadalupe County, and does the current cost split reflect actual usage?
- Should Schertz pursue ESD formation to shift EMS funding from General Fund transfers to a dedicated property tax, insulating core city services?
- What is the collection rate on EMS ambulance billing, and can partnerships with insurance networks or third-party billing improve revenue capture?
