## AI ANALYSIS — Requires Council Review

**Finding:** The Strategic Plan 2024-2025 establishes 'vision, mission, and strategic priorities' but no key performance indicators (KPIs), baseline metrics, or accountability mechanisms are documented. Budget documents do not reference Strategic Plan goal achievement, and no mid-year progress reports are logged.

**Evidence:**
- City of Schertz adopted Strategic Plan for 2024-2025 fiscal years [SOURCE: Strategic Plan 2024-2025, topics/strategic-plan.md]
- The plan establishes vision, mission, and strategic priorities for the city [SOURCE: Strategic Plan 2024-2025, topics/strategic-plan.md]
- Council Strategic Plan Retreat scheduled for February 6, 2026 (2026 Master Calendar) and June 6, 2026 (later calendar version) [SOURCE: 2026 Master Calendar, topics/development.md]
- No KPIs, target metrics, or quarterly dashboards mentioned in any budget document across FY2020-2026
- Budget narratives reference initiatives (Fire Station #4, street improvements) but do not link to Strategic Plan goal achievement

**Comparable Cities:**
- Leading Texas cities (Austin, Plano, Round Rock) publish quarterly 'Strategic Plan Dashboards' with red/yellow/green status indicators. GFOA Distinguished Budget Award criteria require linking budget to strategic goals.

**Suggested Action:** Revise the Strategic Plan by December 2026 to include: (1) 3–5 measurable objectives per priority area with baseline data; (2) annual targets for each KPI; (3) assigned department ownership; (4) quarterly reporting to Council in dashboard format. Examples: 'Reduce average pavement PCI deterioration to <2 points/year,' 'Increase EMS response time compliance to 95% within 8 minutes,' 'Achieve AA+ bond rating renewal.' Integrate Strategic Plan metrics into annual budget presentation and require department directors to report progress at mid-year budget review.

**Council Discussion Questions:**
- Should the Strategic Plan cycle shift to a three-year rolling horizon (vs. two-year) to align with Council terms and budget planning?
- What are the top three strategic priorities for Schertz (e.g., infrastructure, fiscal sustainability, economic development), and how should resources be reallocated to reflect them?
- Who is responsible for monitoring Strategic Plan progress—City Manager, Assistant City Manager, or a dedicated performance management team?
