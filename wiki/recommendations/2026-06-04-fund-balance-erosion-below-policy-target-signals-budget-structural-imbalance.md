## AI ANALYSIS — Requires Council Review

**Finding:** General Fund balance fell to 25% of operating expenditures in FY2024-25, below the city's 26% policy target, despite property tax increases. FY2025-26 shows a $272.7M total budget with General Fund revenues of only $52.8M—a structural gap between recurring expenses and sustainable revenues.

**Evidence:**
- General Fund balance projected at 25% of operating expenditures by end of FY2024-25, below the 26% target [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2024-25, topics/budget.md]
- General Fund maintains 29% fund balance reserve in FY2023-24, exceeding 26% policy—erosion of 4 percentage points in one year [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2023-24, topics/governance.md]
- FY2024-25 General Fund revenues: $49,993,629; expenditures: $52,589,778—a $2.6M structural deficit [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2024-25, topics/budget.md]
- FY2025-26 total budget $272.7M but General Fund revenues only $52.8M—fund balance being used to bridge gap [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26, topics/budget.md]
- Budget adds 26 new positions citywide in FY2025-26 (10 General Fund, 6 Water/Sewer, 4 EMS, 6 Drainage) [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2025-26, topics/governance.md]

**Comparable Cities:**
- GFOA recommends minimum 16.7% (two months) unrestricted fund balance; Schertz's 26% target is prudent but declining trend is concerning. Comparable city data unavailable.

**Suggested Action:** Impose immediate hiring freeze on non-essential General Fund positions until fund balance returns to 26% target. Commission a zero-based budget review of General Fund departments to identify 3–5% in recurring savings. Establish a 'Fund Balance Restoration Surcharge' policy requiring any budget amendment exceeding $100K to include a dedicated revenue source or offsetting cut. Update the FY2026-27 budget to achieve structural balance (recurring revenues ≥ recurring expenses).

**Council Discussion Questions:**
- Should the city reduce the fund balance target to 20% (freeing up $3M for operations) or defend the 26% cushion given DVHS and economic uncertainties?
- Which of the 26 new positions added in FY2025-26 are mission-critical vs. growth-accommodating, and can any be deferred?
- What is the three-year revenue forecast for sales tax (currently 6% growth) and property tax (limited by DVHS), and do projections support current staffing levels?
