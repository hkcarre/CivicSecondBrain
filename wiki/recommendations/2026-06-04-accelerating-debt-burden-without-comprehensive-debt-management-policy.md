## AI ANALYSIS — Requires Council Review

**Finding:** City debt secured by property taxes increased 33% from $66.1M (FY2021-22) to $88.1M (FY2024-25), with a planned $20M Certificate of Obligation issuance in September 2024. No debt-per-capita targets, debt service ratio caps, or multi-year debt capacity analysis are evident in budget documents.

**Evidence:**
- Total debt obligation: $66,084,539 (FY2021-22) → $75,077,338 (FY2020-21) → $88,054,065 (FY2024-25) → $96,572,010 (FY2023-24) → $105,316,557 (FY2022-23) [SOURCE: Multiple budget documents, topics/budget.md]
- City will issue $20 million in Certificates of Obligation for street projects in September 2024 [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2024-25, topics/budget.md]
- Fire Station #4 construction authorized by $15.45 million voter-approved bond (November 2021) [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2023-24, topics/infrastructure.md]
- S&P Global Ratings affirmed AA+ rating in July 2022, but no debt management policy citations in budget documents [SOURCE: Adopted Budget FY2023-24, topics/governance.md]
- Population grew from ~35,121 (FY2019) to 43,969 (FY2025-26), but debt grew faster than population [SOURCE: Financial Summary FY2019, Adopted Budget FY2025-26]

**Comparable Cities:**
- New Braunfels and San Marcos (both 70k+ population) likely have higher total debt but potentially lower per-capita ratios given economies of scale. Benchmark data needed.

**Suggested Action:** Adopt a formal Debt Management Policy by December 2026 establishing: (1) maximum debt service as % of General Fund revenue (recommend 15–20%); (2) debt-per-capita ceiling tied to median household income; (3) annual debt affordability analysis in budget preparation; (4) restrictions on non-voter-approved debt; (5) mandatory 10-year debt retirement schedules for all new issuances. Direct staff to benchmark against GFOA best practices and comparable Texas cities.

**Council Discussion Questions:**
- What is Schertz's current debt service ratio (debt payments ÷ General Fund revenue), and how does it compare to the GFOA's 15% caution threshold?
- Should the city commit to reducing reliance on Certificates of Obligation in favor of voter-approved bonds for major projects?
- What is the 20-year infrastructure replacement liability (streets, water/sewer, drainage), and is current debt capacity adequate?
